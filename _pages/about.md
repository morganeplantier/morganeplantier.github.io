I am an Associate Professor of Economics at the University of Poitiers and a member of the Laboratoire d'économie de Poitiers (LéP).

My research interests lie at the intersection of **labour economics, health economics, and public policy evaluation**. I am particularly interested in how major life events and public policies shape individual trajectories, with a focus on **gender inequalities, parenthood, health, and labour-market outcomes**.

I am also co-director of the [Master Economie Appliquée](https://sceco.univ-poitiers.fr/nos-formations/master-economie-appliquee/) and co-director of the [Diplôme d'Université Economie de la santé](https://sceco.univ-poitiers.fr/nos-formations/diplome-universitaire-economie-de-la-sante-evaluation-medico-economique/) at the University of Poitiers.
